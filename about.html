background

This agri-business was started by three brothers who got the skills from their father in 2004.They learnt how to manage farm activities at their early stages.These later passed the skills to their children who provide cheap labour at the farm . They engage in every farm activity such as planting , weeding,spraying,harvesting among others.

motion

To produce and supply high quality fresh and affordable pineapples while using suitable farming practices and creating income generating opportunities for the local community

vision

To become a leading and trusted pineapple growing enterprise known for quality and suitable products reliable supplier both locally and internationally

Goals

  • To increase production and productivity
  • To improve income and livelihood of farmers
  • To expand the enterprise into pineapple processing and value addition
    • Objectives
    1. To open up a pineapple processing company in kayunga with three years with improved technology and highly skilled labour
    2. To expand the market by 50% in two through providing high quality products especially in the neighbouring countries such as kenya and south sudan
