our plantation

our farm has many pineapple plants which are planted together with bananas so as to carryout mixed farming.This promotes food security for the workers and the rest is sold at the local market provided by our neighbours.

our plants are carried for from planting to harvest .A series of activities is carried out in the due course which include spraying so as to control diseases and pesticides plus some weeds .We also apply fertilizer such as Urea and DAP to the pineapples .

we also apply animals wastes on our banana plants.This is purchased from the neighbouring animal farms
